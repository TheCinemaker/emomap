2. What data we collect, and why

2.1 Account data

When you sign up, we collect your email address and a username you choose. Your password is handled by our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and is never visible to us in plain text. Legal basis: performance of a contract (providing you an account) and consent at sign-up.

2.2 Location data — and how we deliberately limit it

When you post a mood, we read your device's GPS location via your browser's Geolocation API, apply a small random offset (currently ~10 meters) before it ever leaves your device, and store only that approximate point together with your mood and a timestamp. We do this specifically so a single stored point can't be used to pinpoint an exact address. We do not store your raw, un-adjusted GPS coordinates. Legal basis: consent (you must actively grant location permission and choose to post a mood).

2.3 Mood data

Each mood you post (an emotion label + the adjusted location + a timestamp) is visible to other users of the Service as part of the live map — this is the core, intentionally public feature of EmoMap. A mood pin on the map is never labeled with a username, avatar, or any other identity — it is always shown as an anonymous colored point. You only ever have one active mood showing at a time: posting a new one immediately replaces whichever one you had live, and any mood auto-expires after 1 hour regardless. Zoomed-out views blend nearby moods together into a single combined color rather than showing each one separately.

2.4 Profile photo and optional profile details

You may optionally upload a profile photo. You may also optionally fill in extended details — full name, phone number, address, birth date, a contact note, and social media links. None of these optional fields are visible to anyone by default. Each field has its own visibility switch, and even when switched on, it is only ever shown to users you have mutually accepted as friends — never publicly, and this is enforced on our server, not just hidden in the app's interface. Legal basis: consent — providing and sharing these fields is entirely optional and user-controlled.

2.5 Friends and messages

If you send or accept a friend request, we store that connection. If you message a friend, we store the message content, sender, recipient, and timestamps so the conversation can be displayed to both participants. You can only message users you are mutually connected with — not strangers. Legal basis: consent / performance of the specific feature you've chosen to use.

2.6 Photos ("Reels")

You may post a photo that is visible to other users and automatically deleted from view after a short time (currently 20 minutes). Uploaded photos are compressed on your device before upload. Legal basis: consent.

2.7 Venue / business partner data

If your account is enabled as a business partner, you may submit a venue's name, category, exact location, logo, promotional image, description, and a call-to-action link, which are shown publicly on the map to promote your venue. We also record aggregate view and click counts for that listing. Legal basis: performance of a contract (the partner arrangement) and consent.

2.8 Technical and local data

Your language preference, your most recent vote time (to show a cooldown timer), and your login session are stored in your browser's local storage, not on our servers, and never leave your device. Standard web server logs (IP address, browser type, request timestamps) are generated by our hosting and infrastructure providers for security and operational purposes.

4. How long we keep your data

We keep account and mood data for as long as your account is active. Reels photos are removed from public view after 20 minutes but the storage file is deleted on a rolling basis shortly after. If you delete your account (see Section 6), we delete or anonymize your personal data within [RETENTION PERIOD, e.g. 30 days], except where we are legally required to retain certain records for longer (e.g. billing records for partners).

5. Security

We apply several technical safeguards specifically designed around the sensitivity of location data: GPS coordinates are randomized before storage (see 2.2); map views suppress statistics for areas with too few contributors to prevent re-identification; access to the database is governed by row-level security policies so, for example, your optional profile details are only ever readable by accepted friends, enforced at the database level, not just hidden in the app. All traffic between your device and our servers is encrypted (HTTPS).

  • Withdraw consent at any time (e.g. by disabling location access, or removing an optional profile field).
  • Lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority — in Hungary, the National Authority for Data Protection and Freedom of Information (NAIH).
